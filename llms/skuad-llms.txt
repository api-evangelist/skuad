# Skuad

> Skuad (now part of Payoneer as Payoneer Workforce Management) is a global HR and employment platform for hiring, onboarding, paying, and managing full-time employees and contractors across 160+ countries without setting up local legal entities. It provides employer-of-record (EOR) services, international payroll in 70+ currencies, contractor management and payments, benefits administration, background verification, and built-in local compliance. No public developer API or OpenAPI specification was found during enrichment.
